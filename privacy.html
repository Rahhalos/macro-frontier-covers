Privacy Policy

Macro Frontier Publisher transfers Macro Frontier's own videos to Macro Frontier's own social-media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data it handles

An access token for the connected Macro Frontier account, the basic profile fields TikTok returns when that account authorizes the tool (open id, display name, avatar), and the videos being transferred. Nothing else is collected.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored locally on Macro Frontier's own equipment and are never sold, shared, or sent to any third party other than TikTok itself in the course of publishing.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the tool is disconnected. Revoking the app's access from the TikTok account ends all processing.
